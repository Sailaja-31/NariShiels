🛡️ NariShield — Detect. Decide. Protect. 💜

NariShield is a Women's Digital and Personal Safety Website developed to help women identify online scams, detect suspicious messages and fake job offers, recognize manipulation, and access practical safety guidance.

✨ Key Features
🔍 Scam Message Scanner
🚫 Scam Message Guard
🎯 Scam Simulator
💼 Fake Job Checker
🔗 Suspicious Link Checker
💬 Safe Reply Generator
🤖 Safety Assistant
🗺️ Safe Journey Tracker
🆘 Emergency SOS
🔐 Privacy Center

🎯 Our Mission: To empower women through digital awareness, scam prevention, and practical safety resources.
